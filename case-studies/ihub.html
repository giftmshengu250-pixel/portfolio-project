Project Overview

This was a collaboration project where our team recreated the iHub prototype with the goal of making the experience more accessible and user-friendly. We focused on improving how users move through the platform and how easily they could find information they needed.

Problem / Challenge

The main challenge was improving the overall user experience of the prototype. The original structure could make it harder for users to find information quickly and move through the platform without friction. We wanted to simplify navigation and make the experience more intuitive for the people using it.

Our Approach

We focused on clarity, navigation, and usability. By improving how content was organized and making the flow of the interface easier to follow, we aimed to reduce friction and create a smoother experience for users exploring the prototype.

My Contribution / Collaboration

This was a collaborative project, and our team worked together to recreate the experience and improve it for users. I contributed by helping refine the interface and focus on making the platform easier to navigate while keeping the experience accessible and practical.

Outcome / What I Learned

The project reinforced the value of thoughtful navigation and accessibility in digital experiences. It showed how small improvements in layout and structure can make a big difference in usability, and it highlighted the importance of collaboration when trying to solve a user experience problem as a team.
